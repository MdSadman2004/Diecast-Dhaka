# Diecast Dhaka

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Implementation assumption: Vite, vanilla JavaScript, Three.js, Node HTTP and SQLite. No cloud services or uploads. Owner has not specified framework or hosting.

## Users
Confirmed: Bangladeshi Hot Wheels enthusiasts and collectors. Owner resells purchased inventory through the website.

## Product Purpose
A local working resale shop with browse, filters, product viewing, cart, order submission, tracking and owner order/inventory management.

## Positioning
The ordered car, in its selected color and body style, drives through a full-screen 3D sequence into the cart. Interactive collector-garage presentation is user-approved.

## Operating Context
BDT pricing, Bangladesh phone validation, Dhaka/outside delivery zones, cash-on-delivery local demo checkout. Owner needs 30% net profit margin after entered acquisition, packaging, overhead allocation, returns reserve, shipping subsidy and payment fees.

## Capabilities and Constraints
30% margin is a pricing target based on owner inputs, not guaranteed realized profit. Catalog, stock and cost inputs are explicitly demonstrations until owner supplies real SKUs, stock, cost and photos. No paid gateway, supplier API, fulfillment integration or public deployment authorized. New project E:/Diecast-Dhaka. Persistent local orders must be demonstrable. Admin must require a locally configured secret and not expose costs publicly.

## Brand Commitments
User selected collector garage: realistic die-cast cars, racing-orange accents, cinematic studio lighting; aimed at Bangladeshi enthusiasts. Working store name Diecast Dhaka is an implementation assumption, not a confirmed registered business. Independent reseller, not affiliated with Mattel; Hot Wheels and vehicle marks belong to owners.

## Evidence on Hand
No product photography, licensed meshes, actual inventory, supplier prices, customer proof, registered business contacts or courier agreements provided. Procedural vehicle meshes are illustrative silhouettes, not exact product replicas. No invented testimonials or market-value claims.

## Product Principles
- Preserve commercial honesty: distinguish demo assets and orders from real stock and sales.
- Keep shopping usable on mobile and keyboard, and honor reduced motion.
- Calculate price server-side, reserve stock transactionally, persist orders.
- Provide a visible path to buying, without fake scarcity or ratings.
